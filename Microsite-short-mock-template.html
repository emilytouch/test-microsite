FAQs

When will the funds launch?

Only after their registration statements become effective. Sign up to be notified when [TICKER 1], [TICKER 2], [TICKER 3] and [TICKER 4] begin trading.

Where can I buy these funds?

Through most standard brokerage accounts that offer ETFs. No margin account is required.

What's the difference between the four funds?

Each fund seeks a different daily objective on its underlying. Leveraged funds (e.g. 2x) seek to magnify daily moves; inverse funds (e.g. −2x) seek the opposite of the daily move. Use Compare all above to see them side by side.

What does "daily objective" mean?

Each fund's objective applies to a single day only. Over longer periods, compounding can cause returns to differ significantly from the stated multiple of the underlying's return.

Can I lose more than I invest?

No. Unlike borrowing on margin, your maximum loss is limited to the amount you invest. Because of the leverage, you could lose your entire investment, even within a single day.

Are these funds affiliated with the underlying companies?

No. The ETFs and ProShare Advisors LLC are not affiliated with the underlying issuers.
