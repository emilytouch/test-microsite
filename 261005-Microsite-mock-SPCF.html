How they work

1 / 4

  1. Geared funds aim to deliver their multiple, like 2x, from the close of one trading day to the close of the next. Not over weeks or months.

  2. Each day the fund rebalances back to its target. Without it, leverage could drift from 2x to as high as 10x, or down to 1x, making results unpredictable.

  3. Over periods longer than a day, returns can differ significantly from the daily multiple. Higher volatility tends to hurt; lower volatility tends to help. Monitor positions regularly.

  4. Unlike buying on margin or shorting a stock, your loss is limited to what you invest, with no margin calls and no borrowing.

When will SPCF launch?

Only after its registration statement becomes effective. Sign up above to be notified.

Where can I buy SPCF?

Through most standard brokerage accounts that offer ETFs. No margin account is required.

What does SPCF track?

It seeks 2x the daily performance of SpaceX (SPCX), before fees and expenses.

What does "daily objective" mean?

The 2x objective applies to a single day only. Over longer periods, compounding can cause returns to differ significantly from 2x the underlying's return.

Can I lose more than I invest?

No. Unlike borrowing on margin, your maximum loss is limited to the amount you invest. Because of the leverage and SpaceX's post-IPO volatility, you could lose your entire investment, even within a single day.

Is SPCF affiliated with SpaceX?

No. The ETF and ProShare Advisors LLC are not affiliated with the underlying stock issuer.
