Leveraged ETFs (2x, 3x) are designed to magnify the one-day return of a benchmark, a way to express a bullish view.

How they work

1 / 4

  1. Geared funds aim to deliver their multiple, like 2x, from the close of one trading day to the close of the next. Not over weeks or months.

  2. Each day the fund rebalances back to its target. Without it, leverage could drift from 2x to as high as 10x, or down to 1x, making results unpredictable.

  3. Over periods longer than a day, returns can differ significantly from the daily multiple. Higher volatility tends to hurt; lower volatility tends to help. Monitor positions regularly.

  4. Unlike buying on margin or shorting a stock, your loss is limited to what you invest, with no margin calls and no borrowing.
