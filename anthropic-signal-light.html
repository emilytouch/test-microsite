These ProShares ETFs seek daily investment results that correspond, before fees and expenses, to two times (2x) the daily performance of a single stock for ANTU, or negative two times (-2x) for ANTY (each, the “Daily Target”). While each Fund has a daily investment objective, you may hold Fund shares for longer than one day if you believe it is consistent with your goals and risk tolerance. For any holding period other than a day, your return may be higher or lower than the Daily Target. These differences may be significant. Smaller index gains/losses and higher index volatility contribute to returns worse than the Daily Target. Larger index gains/losses and lower index volatility contribute to returns better than the Daily Target. The more extreme these factors are, the more they occur together, and the longer your holding period while these factors apply, the more your return will tend to deviate. Investors should consider periodically monitoring their geared fund investments in light of their goals and risk tolerance.

Answers to common questions about ANTU, ANTY, and how to get 2x leveraged or short exposure to Anthropic (ANTH) stock.

The right Anthropic ETF depends on your view. ANTU targets 2x the daily returns of Anthropic (ANTH) common stock for a bullish view, and ANTY targets -2x for a bearish view. Neither fund has launched yet; ProShares is not a financial advisor and cannot tell you which is best for you.

ANTU is a leveraged single-stock ETF that targets 2x the daily returns of Anthropic (ANTH) common stock, offering 2x leveraged exposure through a single ticker in a standard brokerage account.

Yes. ANTY is an inverse Anthropic ETF that targets -2x the daily returns of Anthropic (ANTH) common stock, built for investors who want to short Anthropic or hedge existing exposure without a margin account.

ANTU is designed to give 2x daily leveraged exposure to Anthropic (ANTH) stock through a single ETF ticker, without borrowing on margin or using options or futures directly.

ANTU seeks to deliver 2x the daily performance of Anthropic primarily through swap agreements and direct holdings of Anthropic (ANTH) shares, rebalanced daily to maintain its target.

ANTH is the ticker for Anthropic's common stock itself. ANTU is a separate ETF that targets 2x ANTH's daily return, so ANTU is expected to move roughly twice as much as ANTH on any given trading day, before fees.

A 2x daily target means the fund aims to deliver twice the performance of Anthropic stock for a single trading day, before fees and expenses. It is not a target for any period longer than one day.

Because the daily target resets every day, gains and losses compound day over day. In choppy or volatile markets this compounding tends to erode returns relative to a simple multiple of Anthropic's return; in strong trending markets it can help. The longer the holding period, the more this effect can matter.

ANTU and ANTY are designed to deliver 2x and -2x daily exposure to Anthropic through a single ticker in a standard brokerage account, without opening a margin account, borrowing shares to short, or meeting margin calls. Maximum loss is generally limited to the amount invested in the fund.
